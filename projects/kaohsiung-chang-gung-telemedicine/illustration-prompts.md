# 友善線性醫療插圖替換清單

> 目前先以 PowerPoint 原生虛線框保留插圖位置。正式插圖生成後，只替換對應 placeholder，不更動文字、圖表、網格或既定 Clinical Calm 視覺系統。所有插圖均不得包含文字、數字、Logo、院徽、病歷或儀表板介面。

## IL-01｜封面：遠距照護連線

```text
Use case: productivity-visual
Asset type: friendly linear medical illustration for a formal 16:9 hospital presentation cover
Primary request: A clinician at a hospital calmly connecting through telemedicine with a community care site, expressed as a simple secure care link.
Style/medium: restrained modern editorial line illustration with small flat color fills, rounded geometry, professional and trustworthy, not cartoonish.
Composition/framing: compact horizontal scene, generous transparent-looking negative space, no background panel.
Color palette: navy #163A5F, medical teal #2A8C8B, pale blue-gray #DCE8F1, provisional orange #F28C28 under 10%, white.
Avoid: text, letters, numbers, logos, hospital emblems, screens with UI, medical records, toy-like characters, 3D clay, neon, exaggerated facial expressions, watermark.
```
